# Cover Letter Draft

Dear Editor,

Please consider the manuscript “Exact Total Domination Numbers of Cylindrical
Grids of Widths Five, Six, and Seven” for publication as a Contribution in
*Discrete Applied Mathematics*.

The paper determines the total domination number of (P_m\square C_n) for
every (n\ge3) and (m\in\{5,6,7\}), including the exceptional values at
width six and width seven. The proof gives a weight-preserving finite-state
closed-walk representation and uses exact min-plus matrix identities to reduce
each infinite family to a finite prefix and an independently replayable finite
certificate.

The subject is within the journal’s scope of algorithmic and applicable
discrete mathematics. The manuscript is original, is not under consideration
elsewhere, and its authors will approve the submitted version; author names,
affiliations, and the corresponding-author details will be completed before
submission. Reproducibility artifacts and the clean-room verifier are included
in the project repository.
