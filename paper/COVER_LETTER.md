# Cover Letter Draft

Dear Editor,

Please consider the manuscript “Efficient Open Domination and Exact Total
Domination in Cylindrical Grids” for publication as a research article in
*Discrete Applied Mathematics*.

The manuscript first gives a complete structural characterization of
efficient open domination in every \(P_m\square C_n\), then derives a
universal total-domination lower bound from the same tridiagonal row system.
An explicit construction proves the sharp family
\(\gamma_t(P_{2k}\square C_{(2k+1)t})=k(k+1)t\) for all \(k,t\ge1\).
Finally, it gives complete total-domination classifications for widths 5, 6,
and 7, including their finite transients, using independently replayable
finite certificates.

The odd-width EOD family is explicitly credited to prior work. The manuscript
does not claim the finite-state/min-plus machinery as a new method; that layer
is used to certify the residual fixed-width propositions. The subject is within
the journal's scope of algorithmic and applicable discrete mathematics. The
manuscript is original, is not under consideration elsewhere, and the supplied
author metadata will be completed before submission. Reproducibility artifacts
are publicly available at \url{https://github.com/coshaman/cylinder}.
