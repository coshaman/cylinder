# DAM Criteria Sources

Access date for all sources: 2026-08-26.

## Official source

- Elsevier, *Discrete Applied Mathematics — Guide for Authors*:
  https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/discrete-applied-mathematics/publish/guide-for-authors
  Used for scope, article type, title-page metadata, abstract and keyword
  limits, source files, highlights, declarations, data policy, and AI-policy
  checks. The detailed local transcription is in
  `DAM_SUBMISSION_REQUIREMENTS.md`.

## Representative journal papers

- *The domination numbers of cylindrical grid graphs*:
  https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0096300310011318
- *2-limited broadcast domination on grid graphs*:
  https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dam.2023.05.031
- *Efficient total domination and related invariants in torus graphs*:
  https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dam.2026.03.047
- *A unified approach for domination and packing problems in graphs*:
  https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dam.2026.06.002

The first two provide close graph-domination and grid exemplars; the latter
two provide current total-domination and general domination exemplars. These
are used only to infer normal referee expectations, not as authority for
submission rules.
