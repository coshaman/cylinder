## Requirements evidenced by the current materials

- Submit an original research contribution within the journal's discrete and
  applied-mathematics scope; the manuscript presents exact graph-parameter
  results and their finite-state verification.
- Supply a title, abstract, keywords, complete author names, affiliations and
  postal addresses, and a designated corresponding author with e-mail.
- Supply the applicable funding, competing-interest, author-contribution,
  acknowledgement, and data/code statements.
- Provide highlights if requested by the live submission workflow. A draft is
  in `HIGHLIGHTS.txt`.
- Provide editable source and the compiled manuscript in the format accepted
  by the live guide. The current source is `manuscript.tex`, with bibliography
  source in `references.bib`.
- Disclose any generative-AI assistance in the form and manuscript where the
  publisher's policy requires it. AI tools are not authors; human authors
  remain responsible for the submitted work.
- Confirm copyright/licence terms at submission. The repository's current
  `LICENSE` is deliberately provisional and is not an author permission.
