# DAM Skeptical Review Rubric

Reviewers score the manuscript independently and are instructed to seek
reasons to reject it rather than optimize for agreement.

| criterion | weight | questions |
|---|---:|---|
| Correctness | 30% | Are definitions, proofs, thresholds, certificates, and exceptions correct? |
| Novelty | 20% | Are widths 5--7 distinct from documented widths 2--4 results? |
| Significance | 15% | Does the paper establish nontrivial exact infinite families? |
| Methodological contribution | 10% | Is the finite-state/min-plus certificate architecture meaningful? |
| Exposition | 10% | Can a graph theorist follow the proof without code? |
| Reproducibility | 10% | Are artifacts, hashes, verifier, and commands sufficient? |
| Venue fit | 5% | Does the work fit DAM’s discrete/applied mathematics scope? |

Allowed verdicts are `STRONG_ACCEPT`, `ACCEPT`, `WEAK_ACCEPT`,
`WEAK_REJECT`, `REJECT`, and `STRONG_REJECT`. `STRONG_ACCEPT` requires no
unresolved fatal or major issue, defensible novelty, publication-quality
exposition, and sufficient public reproducibility.
